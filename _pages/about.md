I am Yusen Peng, an incoming CSE PhD student at The Ohio State University (OSU), starting in Fall 2026. I am fortunate to have been advised by [Dr. Sachin Kumar](https://sites.google.com/view/sachinkumar) since my senior year as an undergraduate student at OSU 🌰!

My research interest is broadly **efficient multimodal LLMs** - How can we make these multimodal LLMs *data efficient* (e.g., token pruning, merging) and *parameter efficient* (e.g., SVD, MoE)? I am currently working on dynamic image tokenization to make vision language models (e.g., [LLaVA](https://github.com/haotian-liu/LLaVA), [QwenVL](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-VL)) more efficient at inference time.

I earned my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from The Ohio State University with [Summa Cum Laude and Honor Research Distinction](https://advising.engineering.osu.edu/node/497/honors-engineering/engineering-honors-distinctions). I completed my [undergrduate thesis](https://kb.osu.edu/items/3479043c-d7b6-4f45-adc6-45779d453742) under the supervision of [Dr. Alper Yilmaz](https://u.osu.edu/pcvlab/) on the topic of transformer-based video understanding ([CascadeFormer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.00692)). Previously, I worked on a variety of AI/ML topics including time series analysis ([pytskit](/assets/pdf/SIGNAL_JMLR.pdf), [TSB-FCST](/assets/pdf/TSB_FCST_nips.pdf)), 3D vision ([SVD^3](/assets/pdf/SVD3_ACCV_draft.pdf)), and mechanistic interpretability ([CE-Bench](https://aclanthology.org/2025.blackboxnlp-1.1)). You can check out my <a href="/assets/pdf/CV_May2026.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">CV</a> here for details.
