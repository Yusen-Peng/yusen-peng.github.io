I am Yusen Peng, an first-year CSE PhD student at The Ohio State University (OSU), starting in Fall 2026. I am incredibly fortunate to be advised by [Dr. Sachin Kumar](https://sites.google.com/view/sachinkumar). My current research interest is **efficient multimodal LLMs** - How can we compress tokens and/or model parameters in a principled way for multimodal LLMs in order to improve both training and inference efficiency?


I earned my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from The Ohio State University with [Summa Cum Laude and Honor Research Distinction](https://advising.engineering.osu.edu/node/497/honors-engineering/engineering-honors-distinctions). Previously, I worked on a variety of AI/ML topics including time series analysis (pytskit, TSB-FCST), 3D vision (SVD^3), action recognition ([CascadeFormer](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-31583-0_28)), and mechanistic interpretability ([CE-Bench](https://aclanthology.org/2025.blackboxnlp-1.1)). You can check out my <a href="/assets/pdf/CV_May2026.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">CV</a> here for details.
